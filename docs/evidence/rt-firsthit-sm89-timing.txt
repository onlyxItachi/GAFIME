Terminology
-----------

The benchmark's literal "ray_rate" field is rows * groups divided by the full
timed score call. It includes launch and programmable CUDA work surrounding
OptiX, so the paper calls it the end-to-end effective ray rate. It is not an
isolated RT-core traversal counter.
