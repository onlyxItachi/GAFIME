  Safe, finite, bounded 2D grouped plans use fixed-function instanced
  triangles. Each axis span must be at least 2^-12 times
  max(1, abs(lo), abs(hi)); triangle bounds expand by eight binary32 ULPs,
  and the any-hit program rechecks the original > and <= predicates.
  Narrow, unbounded, empty, 1D, and 3D plans retain the conservative
  ordered-float custom-AABB path.

  non-overlap. OPTIX_RAY_FLAG_TERMINATE_ON_FIRST_HIT then permits at most one
  accepted contribution per ray, so this mode does not allocate or clear the
  path-by-row duplicate bitset. Ordinary direct mode retains the bitset.
  Double-precision centered target statistics and per-path sums are unchanged.
